Most people who teach launching have studied it. We have done it.

At Travel Likes Me we took products from a blank page to a first order and then to scale, several times over.

We chose the product, designed it with partners in the UK, India and Japan, sourced it from factories in India, China and Vietnam, then listed, launched and scaled it. Then we did it again with the next one. Some launches worked. Some cost us a lot of money. Both taught us more than any course could.

The honest part

It was not a straight line. We burned our hands and lost a lot of money along the way. That is exactly why we are profitable today, and why we can help you avoid the mistakes that cost us.

What founders ask me.

Something else on your mind? Send me a query →

How much money do I need to start a D2C brand?

It depends on what you want to build, and it's very subjective. That's one of the first things we work out together.

Do you take equity or charge a fee?

Both. We normally take a small upfront fee, to see that you're serious about the work, and equity, so we're in the game with you.

Can you help if I only have an idea?

Yes. That is exactly where we start: is there real demand, who are the competitors, do the margins work, and what should the product be? We'll tell you honestly if it is not ready yet.

Do you really travel to China with me?

Yes. Where it makes sense, we can travel with you to the factory. We have travelled to more than 25 countries and worked with factories in India, China and Vietnam.

How long until my first sale?

3 to 6 months, and I can break it down for you on a call.
